Poly Haven preview-scene HDRIs
================================

The offline preview-scene panoramas under assets/preview_scenes are sourced
from Poly Haven and released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0).

License and redistribution statement:
https://polyhaven.com/license

Exact asset pages, download URLs, authors, byte counts, MD5 hashes, and SHA-256
hashes are recorded in assets/preview_scenes/SOURCES.md.

No live Poly Haven API or network access is used by the application at runtime.
